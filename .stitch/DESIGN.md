# Design System — AI Laptop Ecommerce

## Section 1: Color Palette
- **Background:** #0d1117 (very dark charcoal)
- **Surface/Card:** #161b22 (dark slate)
- **Border:** #30363d
- **Primary Accent:** #2b6cee (electric blue)
- **Text Primary:** #e6edf3 (off-white)
- **Text Secondary:** #8b949e (muted gray)
- **Success:** #3fb950 (green)
- **Warning:** #f0883e (orange)
- **Danger:** #f85149 (red)

## Section 2: Typography
- **Font Family:** Space Grotesk (Google Fonts)
- **Heading:** 700 weight, #e6edf3
- **Body:** 400 weight, #e6edf3
- **Caption/Label:** 400 weight, #8b949e

## Section 3: Components
- **Cards:** background #161b22, border 1px #30363d, border-radius 8px, padding 16px
- **Buttons (Primary):** background #2b6cee, border-radius 8px, text white
- **Buttons (Secondary):** background transparent, border 1px #30363d
- **Input Fields:** background #161b22, border 1px #30363d, border-radius 8px
- **Navbar:** background #0d1117, border-bottom 1px #30363d, height 64px

## Section 4: Icons & Imagery
- Use lucide-react or heroicons style icons
- Laptop product images: rectangular, contained in card

## Section 5: Layout
- Max-width: 1280px centered
- Grid: 3-4 columns for product list
- Sidebar: 240px (for admin pages)

## Section 6: Design System Notes for Stitch Generation (REQUIRED - copy into every prompt)
**Design System:**
- Dark theme: background #0d1117, surface/card #161b22
- Font: Space Grotesk (Google Fonts), headings bold
- Accent color: #2b6cee electric blue for buttons & highlights
- Cards: border 1px #30363d, border-radius 8px
- Input fields: dark background, subtle border
- Navigation bar: dark with logo on left, actions on right
- Style: premium, tech-focused, sleek with subtle neon glow effects
