A product detail page for a specific laptop. Shows full product information.

Full page layout with:
1. Breadcrumb navigation (Home > Laptops > Product Name)
2. Left: Large product image gallery (main image + thumbnails)
3. Right: Product title, brand, price (highlighted), specs table (CPU, RAM, Storage, Display), stock badge, quantity selector, 'Thêm Vào Giỏ Hàng' (Add to Cart) button
4. Below: Detailed description, full specifications table
5. Navigation bar at top

**Design System:**
- Dark theme: background #0d1117, surface/card #161b22
- Font: Space Grotesk (Google Fonts), headings bold
- Accent color: #2b6cee electric blue for buttons & highlights
- Cards: border 1px #30363d, border-radius 8px
- Input fields: dark background, subtle border
- Navigation bar: dark with logo on left, actions on right
- Style: premium, tech-focused, sleek with subtle neon glow effects
- Device: Desktop (1280px wide canvas)
